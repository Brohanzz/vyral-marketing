Shield alternatives are what many LinkedIn analytics users search for. This page compares five tools that list LinkedIn analytics, using what each tool's own website says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is one of the five.

When we checked on November 3, 2026, Shield's website did not load, so we state nothing about what it offers or costs. If you use Shield, check its own channels for its current status.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **What the analytics show.** Impressions and engagement are the basics. Some tools add a growth timeline, profile visits or reports.
- **How far back it goes.** Check whether the tool imports your older posts, and what it needs from you to do so.
- **Whether it also writes.** Several tools below add AI writing or a calendar. Decide whether you want that, or analytics alone.
- **Price per profile.** Analytics-only products are often priced per profile.
- **Export.** If you want your data in a spreadsheet, check for CSV export.

## Alternatives to Shield

### AuthoredUp

AuthoredUp centres on the editor. You format posts, see hook suggestions and readability grades, preview the post, then publish or schedule it from a calendar. It also shows impressions, engagement and a growth timeline. It fits creators, agencies and teams that want an editor with analytics.

**Pricing as checked:** Individual $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly) for one profile. Business $14.95 per profile per month for three or more profiles. A custom plan covers 10 or more profiles. Its site says you can try it free with no credit card. [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com)

### Taplio

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool with AI post writing, a daily ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. Its site also lists lead-finding tools. It fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling and outreach in one place.

**Pricing as checked:** Starter $39 a month, Growth $69 a month and Pro $199 a month on monthly billing, with a 7-day free trial. AI post writing starts on the Growth plan. A Team and Agency plan has no price listed. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### Postiv

Postiv plans content each week. Its ideation agent, Bob, builds a plan for each profile from your content pillars and last week's results, and it writes posts when you ask. It schedules to personal profiles, company pages and X, and adds approvals for teams. It fits consultants, B2B teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Creator Pro €49.50 a month for one LinkedIn seat, and Team €159 a month with unlimited members. Agencies talk to sales. Creator Pro and Team start with a 7-day free trial. [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai)

### Supergrow

Supergrow builds a voice profile from your writing and adds AI interviews, a 10 to 15 minute chat that produces several drafts. It repurposes videos, articles and PDFs, schedules with a calendar and queue, and adds approvals, a team dashboard and roles. It fits individuals, teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Pro $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. Teams $199 a month ($189 billed annually) for five accounts. Enterprise is custom. It starts with a 7-day free trial. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### VYRAL

VYRAL reads the numbers on your own LinkedIn posts, shows what worked and drafts your next post from it. The home compares this week with last week. The AI Coach writes from your top posts and scores each draft on six points. You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.

**Pricing:** Creator $49 a month ($39 billed yearly) with 250 AI runs a month. Pro $99 a month ($79 billed yearly) with unlimited AI runs and a fair-use limit of 500 a day.
