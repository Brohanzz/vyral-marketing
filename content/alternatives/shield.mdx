Shield Analytics announced its wind-down in May 2026 after seven years as one of the most trusted LinkedIn analytics tools available. If you are a Shield user looking for where to go next, this page compares the most relevant alternatives directly.

For the full story on what happened to Shield and why, including LinkedIn's broader enforcement pattern against Chrome extension-based tools since 2025, see our companion article: [Shield Analytics Is Shutting Down: What Happened and Where to Go Next](/blog/shield-analytics-shutting-down-alternatives).

## What Shield Did Well

Shield earned its reputation honestly. For seven years it offered post-level impressions tracked over time, engagement breakdowns, audience demographics by job title, location, and company size, and an AI agent (Shield Agent) that let users query their own data in plain language. None of this is available through LinkedIn's native analytics. Shield was, by most accounts, well built and genuinely useful, which is part of why its shutdown is significant. The architecture, not the execution, is what forced closure.

## What to Prioritize in a Replacement

Given how Shield was shut down, specifically because of its Chrome extension architecture, the access method a replacement uses matters more than it might have a year ago. Official LinkedIn API access through OAuth, ideally with formally confirmed partner status, is the only model not targeted by LinkedIn's enforcement actions over the past year. Beyond that, the usual factors apply: analytics depth, whether the tool offers anything beyond reporting, and how well it fits your actual workflow.

## Shield Alternatives Compared

### VYRAL

**Access method:** Official LinkedIn Community Management API, Standard tier.

**What it offers:** Post-level analytics through the official API, plus an AI coaching layer that connects performance data directly to content suggestions, and a strategy feature (Blueprint) that Shield never had. VYRAL is a different category of tool than Shield, part analytics, part content generation, part strategy, rather than a pure analytics replacement.

**Best fit:** Shield users who want analytics connected to a generation and coaching workflow, not just a dashboard.

**Tradeoff:** If you want analytics alone with no AI generation involved, this may be more tool than you need.

### AuthoredUp

**Access method:** Chrome extension reading the LinkedIn page during active sessions (no cookie access, no automation). This is currently tolerated and meaningfully lower-risk than the cookie-based pattern that contributed to Shield's shutdown, though it shares the same general architecture category.

**What it offers:** The strongest editor and formatting tool in this category, plus moderate analytics: post performance, side-by-side comparisons, and basic engagement tracking. Trusted with a 4.8/5 Chrome Web Store rating.

**Best fit:** Shield users who also want best-in-class formatting tools and are comfortable with the DOM-reading extension model.

**Tradeoff:** No AI content generation. Analytics depth does not match what Shield offered for audience demographics specifically.

### MagicPost

**Access method:** Official LinkedIn API, independently confirmed verified developer program partner status.

**What it offers:** Analytics covering impressions, engagement, and follower growth, generated through the official API, alongside AI content generation from a large post library and multi-language support.

**Best fit:** Shield users who want officially compliant analytics paired with straightforward AI generation, without needing a deep strategy layer.

**Tradeoff:** No persistent memory across sessions. Analytics are functional but not as granular as Shield's audience demographic breakdowns.

### Inlytics

**Access method:** Browser automation-adjacent, with confirmed account challenge issues reported from over-requesting, according to independent comparative review.

**What it offers:** A free tier and a budget-friendly paid plan, positioned as a lower-cost Shield alternative.

**Best fit:** Creators specifically prioritizing low cost over data accuracy and compliance certainty.

**Tradeoff:** Documented compliance risk and data accuracy issues compared to Shield. Not recommended for heavy or professional use given the confirmed account challenge pattern.

### LinkedIn Native Analytics

**Access method:** Native, no third-party access required.

**What it offers:** Basic impression and engagement counts directly within LinkedIn, at no cost and with no compliance risk whatsoever, since no external tool is involved.

**Best fit:** Anyone who wants the simplest, lowest-risk option and does not need post-level historical tracking or audience demographic depth.

**Tradeoff:** Far less depth than Shield offered. No historical trend tracking, no demographic breakdowns, no AI querying.

### Buffer or Hootsuite

**Access method:** Native scheduling APIs across multiple platforms.

**What it offers:** Cross-platform scheduling with basic analytics layered in, useful if LinkedIn is one of several channels you manage rather than your primary focus.

**Best fit:** Users who need scheduling across multiple social platforms and only need lightweight LinkedIn analytics as part of that broader workflow.

**Tradeoff:** LinkedIn-specific analytics depth is shallow compared to any of the LinkedIn-first tools above.

## A Note on Exporting Your Shield Data

If you are still a Shield subscriber, export your post history, follower trends, and engagement data before the wind-down completes. Once access ends, that historical data is not recoverable, and no replacement tool, including the ones listed here, can reconstruct Shield's specific historical record for you. Check Shield's official communications directly for the current timeline and export process.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is there a direct, one-to-one replacement for Shield?**
Not exactly. Shield was a dedicated analytics tool with no content creation features. Most alternatives either combine analytics with content generation (VYRAL, MagicPost) or pair lighter analytics with strong editing tools (AuthoredUp). Decide whether you want a pure analytics tool or a combined workflow before choosing.

**Will my Shield data transfer automatically to a new tool?**
No. None of the tools listed here import Shield's historical data directly. Export what you need from Shield before access ends, and expect any new tool to start tracking from the point you connect it.

**Which Shield alternative has the closest analytics depth?**
MagicPost and VYRAL both offer official API-based analytics, though neither currently replicates Shield's specific audience demographic breakdowns (job title, company size) in identical form. AuthoredUp offers solid post-level tracking but through a different access method.

**Is it safe to keep using Shield until it fully shuts down?**
This is a decision based on your own risk tolerance and Shield's official communicated timeline. Export your data as a precaution regardless of your decision, since access loss could happen on Shield's announced schedule.
