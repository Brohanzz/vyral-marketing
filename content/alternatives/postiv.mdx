Postiv alternatives are easy to find, but they are hard to compare because each tool prices and packages things differently. This page lines up Postiv with five other LinkedIn tools, using only what each tool's own site says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is one of the six.

Postiv is built around a weekly content plan, approvals and a single calendar for profiles and company pages. Below you will find what it does well, what to look for in an alternative, and a table with starting prices.

## What Postiv does well

- **A weekly plan.** Postiv's ideation agent, Bob, builds a plan for each profile from your content pillars and last week's results.
- **Ideas.** A daily feed of what is performing in your niche.
- **Drafts and visuals.** A drafts board for posts, carousels and infographics that use your colours, fonts and logo.
- **One calendar.** Personal profiles, company pages and X in one place, with the first comment published alongside each LinkedIn post.
- **Team features.** Approvals per profile, a shared asset library and Slack alerts when a post goes live.
- **Writing on request.** Its site says AI writes posts only if you ask it to, and you can bring your own AI assistant through an MCP server.

Postiv fits consultants, B2B teams, employee advocacy programmes and agencies that want planning, approvals and scheduling together.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **Planning or writing.** Postiv leads with a weekly plan. Other tools lead with writing, an editor or analytics. Decide which you open first each week.
- **Seats and profiles.** Postiv's Creator Pro plan covers one LinkedIn seat. Compare how each tool counts users.
- **Where you publish.** Postiv schedules to LinkedIn and X. If you only post on LinkedIn, a LinkedIn-only tool may cover what you need.
- **Approvals.** If a manager or client signs off posts, check that approvals are on the plan you would buy.
- **Currency and billing.** Postiv and several others price in euro, others in dollars. Check monthly and yearly amounts.

## Alternatives to Postiv

### Taplio

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool with AI post writing, a daily ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. Its site also lists lead-finding tools. It fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling and outreach in one place.

**Pricing as checked:** Starter $39 a month, Growth $69 a month and Pro $199 a month on monthly billing, with a 7-day free trial. AI post writing starts on the Growth plan. A Team and Agency plan has no price listed. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### MagicPost

MagicPost generates posts from a topic, URL or file, can import a writing style, and makes carousels and visuals. It also offers a library of 2M+ posts, scheduling, analytics, an engagement tool and lead detection, plus approval and white-label reports for agencies. It fits individuals, agencies and sales teams.

**Pricing as checked:** In euro: Growth €39 a month or €23 a month billed yearly, Creator €59 or €35, and Agency and Team €49 per seat a month or €39 billed yearly. It has a free trial with no credit card. Analytics are also sold as a separate product, Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in)

### Supergrow

Supergrow builds a voice profile from your writing and adds AI interviews, a 10 to 15 minute chat that produces several drafts. It repurposes videos, articles and PDFs, schedules with a calendar and queue, and adds approvals, a team dashboard and roles. It fits individuals, teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Pro $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. Teams $199 a month ($189 billed annually) for five accounts. Enterprise is custom. It starts with a 7-day free trial. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### Scripe

Scripe is built for company LinkedIn programmes. It drafts posts from colleagues' answers, makes carousels and infographics, sends approval requests by Slack, email or the app, and reports impressions, saves, sends, link clicks, profile views and new followers. It fits agencies and B2B marketing teams of about 11 to 200 people.

**Pricing as checked:** In euro, per month, billed yearly: Solo €55, Advanced €79 and Business €119. Agency pricing is through sales. It is offered to businesses only, and the free trial needs a credit card. [Scripe](https://www.scripe.io)

You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.

**Pricing:** Creator $49 a month ($39 billed yearly) with 250 AI runs a month. Pro $99 a month ($79 billed yearly) with unlimited AI runs and a fair-use limit of 500 a day.
