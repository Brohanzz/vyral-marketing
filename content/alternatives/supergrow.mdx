Supergrow alternatives are worth a look if you want a different price, a different writing workflow or more analytics. This page compares Supergrow with five other LinkedIn tools, using what each tool's own website says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is included.

Supergrow starts from your voice: it builds a profile, runs AI interviews and repurposes videos and articles. Here is how other tools approach the same job.

## What Supergrow does well

- **Voice.** Content DNA builds a profile of your vocabulary, tone, opinions and topics.
- **AI interviews.** A 10 to 15 minute conversation with an AI interviewer produces several draft posts.
- **Repurposing.** Turn YouTube videos, blog posts and PDFs into LinkedIn posts, or speak an idea and get a post.
- **Workflow.** A Kanban board from draft to scheduled, a calendar, a queue and first-comment scheduling.
- **Teams.** Approvals, one dashboard for team voices, per-member insights, challenges with a leaderboard and role-based permissions.
- **Trial.** 7-day free trial.

Supergrow fits individuals building a personal brand, teams that manage several voices, and agencies.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **How you like to start a post.** Supergrow offers an interview and voice input. Others start from a topic, a plan or your past posts.
- **Accounts per plan.** Supergrow's Pro plan includes one account and Teams includes five. Compare account counts for the tools you shortlist.
- **Repurposing.** Supergrow turns videos, articles and PDFs into posts. Check whether others do this.
- **Team workflow.** Supergrow lists approvals, a dashboard, roles and a leaderboard. Check what else is on the team plan of each tool.
- **Analytics.** Supergrow lists visibility, engagement and pipeline impact. Compare how much detail each tool shows.

## Alternatives to Supergrow

### Taplio

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool with AI post writing, a daily ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. Its site also lists lead-finding tools. It fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling and outreach in one place.

**Pricing as checked:** Starter $39 a month, Growth $69 a month and Pro $199 a month on monthly billing, with a 7-day free trial. AI post writing starts on the Growth plan. A Team and Agency plan has no price listed. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### Postiv

Postiv plans content each week. Its ideation agent, Bob, builds a plan for each profile from your content pillars and last week's results, and it writes posts when you ask. It schedules to personal profiles, company pages and X, and adds approvals for teams. It fits consultants, B2B teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Creator Pro €49.50 a month for one LinkedIn seat, and Team €159 a month with unlimited members. Agencies talk to sales. Creator Pro and Team start with a 7-day free trial. [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai)

### MagicPost

MagicPost generates posts from a topic, URL or file, can import a writing style, and makes carousels and visuals. It also offers a library of 2M+ posts, scheduling, analytics, an engagement tool and lead detection, plus approval and white-label reports for agencies. It fits individuals, agencies and sales teams.

**Pricing as checked:** In euro: Growth €39 a month or €23 a month billed yearly, Creator €59 or €35, and Agency and Team €49 per seat a month or €39 billed yearly. It has a free trial with no credit card. Analytics are also sold as a separate product, Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in)

### AuthoredUp

AuthoredUp centres on the editor. You format posts, see hook suggestions and readability grades, preview the post, then publish or schedule it from a calendar. It also shows impressions, engagement and a growth timeline. It fits creators, agencies and teams that want an editor with analytics.

**Pricing as checked:** Individual $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly) for one profile. Business $14.95 per profile per month for three or more profiles. A custom plan covers 10 or more profiles. Its site says you can try it free with no credit card. [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com)

The AI Coach writes from your top posts and scores each draft on six points. On Pro, patterns compare your hook types, timing and themes with your own average, and the Data Analyst answers questions about your numbers. You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.

**Pricing:** Creator $49 a month ($39 billed yearly) with 250 AI runs a month. Pro $99 a month ($79 billed yearly) with unlimited AI runs and a fair-use limit of 500 a day.
