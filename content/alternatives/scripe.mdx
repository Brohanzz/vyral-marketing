Scripe alternatives matter most to teams and agencies that run LinkedIn for several people. This page compares Scripe with five other LinkedIn tools, using what each tool's own website says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is one of the six.

Scripe is aimed at B2B marketing teams and agencies, and its plans are priced for businesses. If you are a solo creator, or you want a tool built mainly around your own analytics, the alternatives below may suit you better.

## What Scripe does well

- **Drafts from your colleagues.** Scripe drafts posts from colleagues' answers to questions, so an expert can answer by voice and get a post back.
- **Visuals.** Carousels, infographics and quote cards in a brand kit.
- **Approvals.** Approval requests go out by Slack, email or the app, and every post starts as a draft.
- **Calendar.** One content calendar for the company page and colleagues' posts, with Slack alerts when a post goes live.
- **Analytics.** Impressions, saves, sends, link clicks, profile views and new followers per post and profile, plus tracked links.

Scripe fits agencies and B2B marketing teams of about 11 to 200 people that run LinkedIn for several colleagues.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **Who the tool is for.** Scripe is offered to businesses only. Some tools below also sell to individuals.
- **Seats and roles.** Scripe lists extra full seats at €45 a month and light seats at €20 a month on some plans. Compare how each tool prices extra people.
- **Approvals.** Check where approvals sit and how requests reach people: Slack, email or the app.
- **Reporting.** Scripe lists impressions, saves, sends, link clicks, profile views and new followers. Check what each alternative reports.
- **Trial.** Scripe's free trial needs a credit card and its length is not stated. Compare trial terms.

## Alternatives to Scripe

### Postiv

Postiv plans content each week. Its ideation agent, Bob, builds a plan for each profile from your content pillars and last week's results, and it writes posts when you ask. It schedules to personal profiles, company pages and X, and adds approvals for teams. It fits consultants, B2B teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Creator Pro €49.50 a month for one LinkedIn seat, and Team €159 a month with unlimited members. Agencies talk to sales. Creator Pro and Team start with a 7-day free trial. [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai)

### Supergrow

Supergrow builds a voice profile from your writing and adds AI interviews, a 10 to 15 minute chat that produces several drafts. It repurposes videos, articles and PDFs, schedules with a calendar and queue, and adds approvals, a team dashboard and roles. It fits individuals, teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Pro $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. Teams $199 a month ($189 billed annually) for five accounts. Enterprise is custom. It starts with a 7-day free trial. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### MagicPost

MagicPost generates posts from a topic, URL or file, can import a writing style, and makes carousels and visuals. It also offers a library of 2M+ posts, scheduling, analytics, an engagement tool and lead detection, plus approval and white-label reports for agencies. It fits individuals, agencies and sales teams.

**Pricing as checked:** In euro: Growth €39 a month or €23 a month billed yearly, Creator €59 or €35, and Agency and Team €49 per seat a month or €39 billed yearly. It has a free trial with no credit card. Analytics are also sold as a separate product, Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in)

### Taplio

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool with AI post writing, a daily ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. Its site also lists lead-finding tools. It fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling and outreach in one place.

**Pricing as checked:** Starter $39 a month, Growth $69 a month and Pro $199 a month on monthly billing, with a 7-day free trial. AI post writing starts on the Growth plan. A Team and Agency plan has no price listed. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### VYRAL

VYRAL reads the numbers on your own LinkedIn posts, shows what worked and drafts your next post from it. The home compares this week with last week. The AI Coach writes from your top posts and scores each draft on six points. On Pro, patterns compare your hook types, timing and themes with your own average, and the Data Analyst answers questions about your numbers. You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.

**Pricing:** Creator $49 a month ($39 billed yearly) with 250 AI runs a month. Pro $99 a month ($79 billed yearly) with unlimited AI runs and a fair-use limit of 500 a day.
