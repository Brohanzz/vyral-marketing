Taplio alternatives are worth a look when you want a different mix of writing help, analytics, scheduling or price. This page compares Taplio with five other LinkedIn tools using what each tool's own website says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product, and it is one of the tools listed.

Taplio covers a lot: AI writing, an ideas feed, scheduling, engagement tools and lead tools. If you want fewer parts, a lower price, a team workflow or a stronger focus on your own post analytics, one of the tools below may fit better.

## What Taplio does well

- **AI post creation.** You describe what you want to say and Taplio turns it into a post in your voice, with hooks, rewrites and tone matching.
- **Ideas.** A daily feed of posts ranked for your niche, and reach prediction before you publish.
- **Scheduling.** Batch a week of posts, use a visual queue, post at times tuned to your followers, and schedule carousels and series.
- **Engagement tools.** A curated feed of people to comment on, and AI comment starters.
- **Lead tools.** Find prospects by title, industry and engagement. Outreach automation is listed on the Pro plan.
- **For AI assistants.** Taplio MCP V2 adds an Engage toolset for Claude, ChatGPT and other MCP-compatible assistants.
- **Learning.** Free Academy masterclasses, a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back policy on the first payment.

Taplio fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling, engagement and lead tools in one place.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **The job you need done most.** Writing posts, planning a week, tracking results and finding leads are different jobs. Pick the tool that is built around yours.
- **What is on which plan.** AI post writing starts on Taplio's Growth plan, so compare the plan you would actually buy, not the cheapest one.
- **How many profiles.** Some tools price per profile or per seat. Count the people who will post.
- **Analytics you will read.** Check whether the numbers explain why a post worked, or only report totals.
- **Trial terms.** Most tools in this list offer a free trial. Check the length and whether a card is needed.
- **Add-ons you may not need.** Taplio lists lead outreach, comment tools and an API. If you only want to write and measure, a simpler plan elsewhere may be enough.

## Alternatives to Taplio

### AuthoredUp

AuthoredUp centres on the editor. You format posts, see hook suggestions and readability grades, preview the post, then publish or schedule it from a calendar. It also shows impressions, engagement and a growth timeline. It fits creators, agencies and teams that want an editor with analytics.

**Pricing as checked:** Individual $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly) for one profile. Business $14.95 per profile per month for three or more profiles. A custom plan covers 10 or more profiles. Its site says you can try it free with no credit card. [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com)

### MagicPost

MagicPost generates posts from a topic, URL or file, can import a writing style, and makes carousels and visuals. It also offers a library of 2M+ posts, scheduling, analytics, an engagement tool and lead detection, plus approval and white-label reports for agencies. It fits individuals, agencies and sales teams.

**Pricing as checked:** In euro: Growth €39 a month or €23 a month billed yearly, Creator €59 or €35, and Agency and Team €49 per seat a month or €39 billed yearly. It has a free trial with no credit card. Analytics are also sold as a separate product, Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in)

### Supergrow

Supergrow builds a voice profile from your writing and adds AI interviews, a 10 to 15 minute chat that produces several drafts. It repurposes videos, articles and PDFs, schedules with a calendar and queue, and adds approvals, a team dashboard and roles. It fits individuals, teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Pro $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. Teams $199 a month ($189 billed annually) for five accounts. Enterprise is custom. It starts with a 7-day free trial. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### Postiv

Postiv plans content each week. Its ideation agent, Bob, builds a plan for each profile from your content pillars and last week's results, and it writes posts when you ask. It schedules to personal profiles, company pages and X, and adds approvals for teams. It fits consultants, B2B teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Creator Pro €49.50 a month for one LinkedIn seat, and Team €159 a month with unlimited members. Agencies talk to sales. Creator Pro and Team start with a 7-day free trial. [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai)

The AI Coach writes from your top posts and scores each draft on six points. On Pro, patterns compare your hook types, timing and themes with your own average, and the Data Analyst answers questions about your numbers. You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.
