MagicPost alternatives are worth a look if you want a different price, a different team set-up or deeper analytics. This page compares MagicPost with five other LinkedIn tools, using what each tool's own website says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is included.

MagicPost lists a wide set of features and plans for individuals, agencies and teams. The comparison below shows where other tools differ.

## What MagicPost does well

- **Writing.** An AI post generator that works from a topic, URL or file, lets you import a writing style, and makes carousels, visuals and AI photos.
- **Inspiration.** A library of 2M+ posts to browse, hook generation and an option to analyse a creator.
- **Publishing.** Publish and schedule, with analytics for impressions and followers and recommendations based on the data.
- **Engagement.** An engagement tool for replying to comments and building lists of creators to comment on, plus lead detection.
- **Agencies.** Client management, approval and white-label reports.
- **Connected assistants.** An MCP server and an API.

MagicPost fits individuals, solopreneurs, agencies and ghostwriters, sales teams and employee advocacy teams.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **Plan for how you work.** MagicPost has Growth and Creator plans for individuals, and Agency and Team plans priced per seat. Match the plan to how many profiles you manage.
- **Where analytics sit.** MagicPost sells analytics as part of its plans and also as a separate product, Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. Check what is included.
- **Writing style.** MagicPost lets you import a style. Other tools learn from your past posts or from an interview.
- **Euro or dollars.** MagicPost prices in euro. Several alternatives price in dollars.
- **Trial.** MagicPost lists a free trial with no credit card and does not state its length. Ask before you rely on it.

## Alternatives to MagicPost

### Taplio

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool with AI post writing, a daily ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. Its site also lists lead-finding tools. It fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling and outreach in one place.

**Pricing as checked:** Starter $39 a month, Growth $69 a month and Pro $199 a month on monthly billing, with a 7-day free trial. AI post writing starts on the Growth plan. A Team and Agency plan has no price listed. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### Postiv

Postiv plans content each week. Its ideation agent, Bob, builds a plan for each profile from your content pillars and last week's results, and it writes posts when you ask. It schedules to personal profiles, company pages and X, and adds approvals for teams. It fits consultants, B2B teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Creator Pro €49.50 a month for one LinkedIn seat, and Team €159 a month with unlimited members. Agencies talk to sales. Creator Pro and Team start with a 7-day free trial. [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai)

### Supergrow

Supergrow builds a voice profile from your writing and adds AI interviews, a 10 to 15 minute chat that produces several drafts. It repurposes videos, articles and PDFs, schedules with a calendar and queue, and adds approvals, a team dashboard and roles. It fits individuals, teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Pro $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. Teams $199 a month ($189 billed annually) for five accounts. Enterprise is custom. It starts with a 7-day free trial. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### AuthoredUp

AuthoredUp centres on the editor. You format posts, see hook suggestions and readability grades, preview the post, then publish or schedule it from a calendar. It also shows impressions, engagement and a growth timeline. It fits creators, agencies and teams that want an editor with analytics.

**Pricing as checked:** Individual $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly) for one profile. Business $14.95 per profile per month for three or more profiles. A custom plan covers 10 or more profiles. Its site says you can try it free with no credit card. [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com)

### VYRAL

VYRAL reads the numbers on your own LinkedIn posts, shows what worked and drafts your next post from it. The home compares this week with last week. The AI Coach writes from your top posts and scores each draft on six points. On Pro, patterns compare your hook types, timing and themes with your own average, and the Data Analyst answers questions about your numbers. You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.

**Pricing:** Creator $49 a month ($39 billed yearly) with 250 AI runs a month. Pro $99 a month ($79 billed yearly) with unlimited AI runs and a fair-use limit of 500 a day.
