Kleo alternatives are worth comparing if you want a different price, a different way of writing, or more analytics. This page lines up Kleo with five other LinkedIn tools, using what each tool's own website says. Prices were checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is one of the six.

Kleo combines writing help with a training library, templates and a community. That mix suits some people very well. Others want a lighter tool, or one with more on analytics.

## What Kleo does well

- **A full growth system.** Kleo describes itself as more than an AI writing tool, covering ideation, writing, design, scheduling and analytics.
- **Your style.** It says it learns your expertise, style, audience and past content.
- **Graphics.** Up to 20 generated visuals a month, plus a swipe file and knowledge base.
- **Learning and community.** A training library, 400+ templates, 200+ post examples, weekly live calls and a private community.
- **Languages.** Posts can be generated in English (UK and US), German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish and Czech.

Kleo fits consultants, advisors, coaches, creators and founders who want writing help plus training and community.

## What to look for in an alternative

- **Tool or programme.** Kleo bundles software with training, live calls and a community. Decide whether you want the learning part.
- **Profiles.** Kleo lists extra profiles at $49 a month each, and multi-profile support for ghostwriters and agencies as coming soon. Check how each alternative handles more than one profile.
- **Price shape.** Kleo shows a monthly price and an annual price. Compare the yearly total across tools.
- **Scheduling.** Kleo lists scheduling for LinkedIn. Check that the tool you pick schedules where you post.
- **Analytics.** Kleo lists performance insights. Look at how much detail each alternative gives.

## Alternatives to Kleo

### Taplio

Taplio is a LinkedIn growth tool with AI post writing, a daily ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. Its site also lists lead-finding tools. It fits creators, founders and sales teams who want writing, scheduling and outreach in one place.

**Pricing as checked:** Starter $39 a month, Growth $69 a month and Pro $199 a month on monthly billing, with a 7-day free trial. AI post writing starts on the Growth plan. A Team and Agency plan has no price listed. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### AuthoredUp

AuthoredUp centres on the editor. You format posts, see hook suggestions and readability grades, preview the post, then publish or schedule it from a calendar. It also shows impressions, engagement and a growth timeline. It fits creators, agencies and teams that want an editor with analytics.

**Pricing as checked:** Individual $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly) for one profile. Business $14.95 per profile per month for three or more profiles. A custom plan covers 10 or more profiles. Its site says you can try it free with no credit card. [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com)

### MagicPost

MagicPost generates posts from a topic, URL or file, can import a writing style, and makes carousels and visuals. It also offers a library of 2M+ posts, scheduling, analytics, an engagement tool and lead detection, plus approval and white-label reports for agencies. It fits individuals, agencies and sales teams.

**Pricing as checked:** In euro: Growth €39 a month or €23 a month billed yearly, Creator €59 or €35, and Agency and Team €49 per seat a month or €39 billed yearly. It has a free trial with no credit card. Analytics are also sold as a separate product, Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in)

### Supergrow

Supergrow builds a voice profile from your writing and adds AI interviews, a 10 to 15 minute chat that produces several drafts. It repurposes videos, articles and PDFs, schedules with a calendar and queue, and adds approvals, a team dashboard and roles. It fits individuals, teams and agencies.

**Pricing as checked:** Pro $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. Teams $199 a month ($189 billed annually) for five accounts. Enterprise is custom. It starts with a 7-day free trial. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### VYRAL

VYRAL reads the numbers on your own LinkedIn posts, shows what worked and drafts your next post from it. The home compares this week with last week. The AI Coach writes from your top posts and scores each draft on six points. On Pro, patterns compare your hook types, timing and themes with your own average, and the Data Analyst answers questions about your numbers. You can publish and schedule from VYRAL, and it is built on LinkedIn's official API. VYRAL focuses on LinkedIn only. It fits creators, ghostwriters and agencies, and Business covers one company page.

**Pricing:** Creator $49 a month ($39 billed yearly) with 250 AI runs a month. Pro $99 a month ($79 billed yearly) with unlimited AI runs and a fair-use limit of 500 a day. Business is set up with us. Every account starts with 14 days of Pro, including 50 AI runs, with no credit card. See [pricing](/pricing).
