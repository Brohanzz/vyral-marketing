LinkedIn analytics tools differ in what they show, in what else they do and in what they cost. This page lists nine tools that mention analytics on their own websites, in alphabetical order. We read each tool's site and pricing page on November 3, 2026 and list only what they say. VYRAL is our product and is one of the nine.

## How to read this list

A tool earns a place here if its own site mentions LinkedIn analytics. We do not rank them. Which one is right depends on whether you want analytics alone, analytics with an editor, or analytics that feed your writing.

## The tools

### AuthoredUp

An editor with analytics next to it: impressions and engagement, a growth timeline, analysis of individual posts and team analytics. Individual is $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly). [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com)

### Kleo

A LinkedIn growth system that lists performance insights alongside writing, graphics and scheduling. Standard is $99 a month. [Kleo](https://kleo.so)

### MagicPost

Lists impressions and followers with data-backed recommendations, inside plans from €39 a month. Analytics are also sold on their own as Kliver Analytics, at €19 a month per profile. [MagicPost](https://magicpost.in)

### Postiv

Reach, engagement, followers and profile visits for each post and profile. Creator Pro is €49.50 a month. [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai)

### Scripe

Impressions, saves, sends, link clicks, profile views and new followers per post and profile, plus tracked links. Plans start at €55 a month billed yearly and are offered to businesses. [Scripe](https://www.scripe.io)

### Supergrow

Visibility, engagement and pipeline impact, with weekly reports and team-wide analytics on its Teams plan. Pro is $39 a month. [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai)

### Taplio

Core analytics on its Starter plan, which is $39 a month on monthly billing. It also lists AI writing from the Growth plan, an ideas feed and engagement tools. [Taplio](https://taplio.com)

### Typefully

Lists analytics for posts on the networks it supports, including LinkedIn. We could not read its pricing page, so we list no price. [Typefully](https://typefully.com)

### VYRAL

The home compares this week with last week. On Pro, patterns compare your hook types, timing and themes with your own average, and the Data Analyst answers questions about your numbers. The Coach writes from your top posts. Creator is $49 a month ($39 billed yearly), and every account starts with 14 days of Pro. See [LinkedIn analytics](/linkedin-analytics) and the [pricing](/pricing).
