This guide is for people moving their LinkedIn analytics from one tool to another, including people leaving Shield. It starts with downloading your own data from LinkedIn, then covers what to look for in a new tool and compares five tools that list LinkedIn analytics. Everything about other tools comes from those tools' own websites, checked on November 3, 2026. VYRAL is our product and is one of the five.

## Download your own data from LinkedIn first

Before you switch tools, take a copy of what LinkedIn holds about you. These are the steps from [LinkedIn's help page on downloading your account data](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1339364/downloading-your-account-data):

1. Click the **Me** icon at the top of your LinkedIn homepage.
2. Choose **Settings & Privacy** in the dropdown.
3. Select **Data Privacy** in the left-hand menu.
4. Under **How LinkedIn uses your data**, pick **Download your data**.
5. Choose the data you want, then select **Request archive**.

You can pick specific categories or ask for a larger archive. LinkedIn says it emails you when the file is ready: within minutes for specific categories, and within about 24 hours for the larger archive, though its own category tables show some taking longer. The download link stays active for 72 hours. LinkedIn says the feature is not available on mobile and recommends a personal computer.

Only categories that apply to your account appear. The examples LinkedIn lists include Profile, Positions, Connections, Messages, Skills, Articles and Learning. Some connections' email addresses may be missing if those members chose not to share them. LinkedIn does not include other members' personal data, "People You May Know" data or "Who's Viewed Your Profile" data.

## What LinkedIn analytics tools usually cover

Most tools show impressions, reactions and comments for each post. Some add followers, profile visits, a growth timeline or best times to post. The differences that matter are how much detail you get per post, whether the tool explains what changed, and whether it does anything with the numbers afterwards, such as helping you write the next post.

## What to look for when you move

- **History.** Ask what the new tool imports when you sign up, how far back it goes, and what you need to do to add older posts.
- **The same numbers.** If you want to compare trends, check that the new tool reports the metrics you tracked before, such as impressions and reactions.
- **How numbers arrive.** Some tools refresh numbers on a schedule. Others only take what you upload. Ask which one applies.
- **Text of old posts.** An upload of numbers may not include what the posts said. If you want the wording kept, keep your own archive.
- **Export.** Check whether you can export your data from the new tool, in case you move again.
- **Price per profile.** Analytics-only products are often priced per profile, while writing tools often price per plan.
- **A trial.** Use the free trial on your own posts before you cancel anything else.

## Tools that list LinkedIn analytics

AuthoredUp lists a dashboard overview of your LinkedIn performance, impressions and engagement, a growth timeline and analysis of individual posts. It also has an editor, a calendar and saved posts you can export to CSV. Individual is $19.95 a month ($16.63 a month billed yearly) for one profile. See [AuthoredUp](https://authoredup.com).

### Taplio

Taplio lists core analytics on its Starter plan ($39 a month on monthly billing), with AI post writing from the Growth plan ($69 a month). It also lists an ideas feed, scheduling and engagement tools. See [Taplio](https://taplio.com).

### Postiv

Postiv lists reach, engagement, followers and profile visits per post and profile, and says next week's plan starts from your top posts. Creator Pro is €49.50 a month for one LinkedIn seat. See [Postiv](https://www.postiv.ai).

### Supergrow

Supergrow lists visibility, engagement and pipeline impact, weekly reports per member and per team, and team-wide analytics on its Teams plan. Pro is $39 a month ($31 a month billed annually) for one account. See [Supergrow](https://www.supergrow.ai).
