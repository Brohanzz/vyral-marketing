Before you connect a LinkedIn tool to your account, it helps to know how the tool connects and what it can do once it is connected. This guide explains the common ways, gives you a checklist you can use on any tool, and then describes how VYRAL connects. It does not rate or name any other tool.

## How LinkedIn tools can connect to your account

Tools reach your LinkedIn account in a few different ways. Each one gives the tool different access.

### Signing in with LinkedIn

You are sent to LinkedIn's own page, you choose which permissions to give, and the tool receives access through LinkedIn's APIs. The tool never sees your password, and you can remove its access in your LinkedIn settings. LinkedIn publishes these APIs for third-party apps.

### A browser extension

An extension runs inside your browser and works with the pages you open on LinkedIn. What it can read or do depends on the permissions you accept when you install it.

### Automation

Some software performs actions on your account on a schedule or in response to rules, for example liking, commenting, sending connection requests or messaging. If a tool does this, check which actions it takes and how often.

### Sharing login details

A few tools ask for your password or for session details. You should not need to give either to a tool that signs in through LinkedIn.

LinkedIn's own documentation and User Agreement describe what third parties may do. Read them for the full rules.

## A checklist for judging any tool

Use these questions on any LinkedIn tool, whatever it claims.

1. **How does it sign in?** Through LinkedIn's own page, or by asking for your password?
2. **Which permissions does it ask for?** Do they match what the tool does? Reading analytics is different from publishing.
3. **Can you remove its access?** You should be able to do it in your LinkedIn settings, without contacting the tool.
4. **Does it act on your account on its own?** Check for automatic likes, comments, connection requests or messages, and whether you can switch them off.
5. **What does it store, and where?** Look for a privacy policy that says what data it keeps and for how long.
6. **Can you delete your data?** Check how to delete your account and what happens to your data after.
7. **Who runs it?** Look for a named company, an address, a security page and a data processing agreement if you are a business.
8. **What does your plan include?** Check limits, trial terms and cancellation before you pay.

## How VYRAL connects

- **Sign-in.** You connect through LinkedIn's own login, so VYRAL never sees your password.
- **Permissions.** LinkedIn uses separate permissions for publishing and for analytics, so you connect twice. One lets VYRAL publish the posts you choose, and the other reads the numbers on your posts.
- **Actions.** VYRAL publishes when you click Publish or schedule a post. It never automates likes, comments or connection requests, and it adds nothing to your browser.
- **Access.** You can remove VYRAL's access any time in your LinkedIn settings.
- **Data.** Your data is encrypted at rest and in transit. We never share or sell it. You can delete your account and its data from Settings.
